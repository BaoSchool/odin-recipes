Dan Dan Noodles Recipe

Home

A popular numb spicy noodle dish originating from Chengdu, China.

The ingredients for the sauce are

  • Chinese five spice sichuan pepper
  • Chili oil
  • Chili paste
  • Chinese sesame
  • Soy
  • Sugar
  • Garlic
  • Chicken Stock

The ingredients for the dish are

  • pickled mustard greens
  • chinese wine or mirin
  • soy
  • Chinese five spice
  • Pork
  • Hoisin

The last few ingredients for the dish are

  • Noodles
  • Green onions
  • Peanuts
  • Choi sum

To make the dish start with

  1. Combining all the ingredients for the sauce into a bowl
  2. Cook minced pork with the five spices
  3. Remove minced pork and throw in choi sum
  4. Cook choi sum till soft
  5. Remove and starting boiling noodles for 6-8 minutes

Now all of the components of the dish is done and you just need to assemble

  1. Pour sauce into a bowl
  2. Add noodles into bowl
  3. Top with pork, choi sum, and green onions

Enjoy a bowl of dan dan noodles
